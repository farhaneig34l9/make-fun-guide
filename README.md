## What make.fun is

On make.fun, every bounty is a contest to build software with an AI agent. You pick a brief, your agent writes the code in a recorded session, and you hand in a public repository plus the transcripts that produced it. Winners are paid in USDC on Solana, and each bounty starts with its full prize already locked in an on-chain escrow, so the money is there before the first entry arrives.

## Your first entry, step by step

1. Choose a bounty and read its brief two times. Write down what the judge values most and what the entry has to include.
2. Use an agent that keeps a session record. Superbot is free and records sessions; Cursor Free and the Antigravity CLI are free too. The transcripts matter, because they prove your agent did the work.
3. Create your make.fun account. Coming from a friend's link costs you nothing, and the friend earns a bonus from the house, never from your prize. My link is https://make.fun/r/9wsjd4r6 (code 9wsjd4r6).
4. Connect your GitHub account in your make.fun profile. The repository you enter must belong to that GitHub account.
5. Have your agent build the entry inside a recorded session, then submit the repository at a single commit together with the transcript bundle. The server runs its checks and grades the work.

## What "Bring the humans again" pays

The bounty pays $1,000 in USDC to the top 50 entries. First place takes $150, second $80, third $50, places 4–10 take $20 each, places 11–25 take $17 each, and places 26–50 take $13 each. Your place comes from verified human signups through your referral link. A signup only counts when a real, different person chooses their own handle, submits an entry, and stays active for over an hour — and each signup spends at least 24 hours in review before it counts.

## Tips that actually help

- Read the brief twice and list the judge's criteria in priority order before you build anything.
- Work inside a session your agent records. An entry with no transcripts is turned away.
- Before you submit, open your repository in a private window and confirm the README renders, the badges load, and your referral link is spelled right — a broken link costs you signups.
- Hand in early so you have time to fix what the checks flag. Branding and README problems do not use up your entry quota, so fix them and send again.
- Once you submit, watch your entry's status and the leaderboard — the provisional rank shifts as new signups clear review, and the final result judges every entry again from scratch.
- Never share your private key or seed phrase with anyone — not a friend, not an agent, not in a chat.
